## Description
DealGo makes getting around and ordering food fast and transparent.

RIDES YOUR WAY
• Motorbike, 4-seat or 7-seat car.
• Suggest a price; nearby drivers send offers and you pick the one you like.
• See driver name, vehicle and rating before you confirm.
• Live tracking while your driver approaches, plus a pickup code so you board the right vehicle.

FOOD FROM LOCAL SPOTS
• Browse stores and menus with photos.
• Follow your order from the kitchen to your door; confirm delivery with a code.

FOR DRIVERS AND MERCHANTS
• Drivers: go online/offline, accept rides or deliveries, see earnings.
• Merchants: accept orders, manage menu, opening hours and daily revenue.
• Partners must complete verification (KYC/KYB) first.

SAFETY & PRIVACY
• In-app chat and voice calls with your driver, without sharing phone numbers.
• Delete your account in the app (Account → Delete account).
